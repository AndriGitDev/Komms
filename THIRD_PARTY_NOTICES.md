# Third-party notices

Komms project-authored material defaults to AGPL-3.0-only as described in
[License, Trademark, and Asset Policy](docs/47-license-trademark-assets.md).
The following material is retained in the repository under its own license.

## BIP-39 English word list

`crates/kult-crypto/src/wordlist.rs` embeds the 2,048-word English vocabulary
from BIP-39. BIP-39 identifies the specification and reference material as
MIT-licensed. The retained license notice is
[`LICENSES/BIP-39-MIT.txt`](LICENSES/BIP-39-MIT.txt).

The complete machine-readable asset inventory is
[`operations/v1/assets.json`](operations/v1/assets.json). Dependency licenses
remain those of the corresponding locked packages and are inventoried by the
release evidence controls.
